# Shannon Game: Measuring the Entropy of English

In 1951 Claude Shannon measured the entropy of printed English, how much information each letter carries, by having people guess a hidden text one letter at a time. This site lets you play his game, gives your own estimate in bits per letter, and compares it with his and with later measurements.

**Play:** https://rahman-tawhid.github.io/shannon-game/
